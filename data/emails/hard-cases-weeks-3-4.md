# Hard cases planted in weeks 3 and 4 (E-0451 to E-0750)

Each row is an email whose correct handling needs more than the obvious reading: a policy edge, a missing fact, a second issue, a trap for an AI reader. The last column says what a correct reply must or must not do, by the policies in `docs/applied-world.md` and the help centre. Order IDs from TW-10301 up are new (odd numbers; not in `orders.db`). Rows marked ATTACK carry `attack: true`.

Note: no basket of catalogue items at sale prices comes to exactly ₹2,000, so the exact-₹2,000 free-shipping edge appears as a customer who is ₹2 short (E-0716) and as the help-centre rule in the replies.
