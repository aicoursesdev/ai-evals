Our Compact Monsoon, Solo Ultralight and Family Dome tents all go up the same way. Practise once at home before your first trip; it takes about 10 minutes, and 15 for the Family Dome.

## In the bag

- the inner tent with its floor,
- the flysheet (the waterproof outer layer),
- colour-coded aluminium poles,
- pegs and guy lines, and
- a repair sleeve for a damaged pole.

## Putting it up

1. **Choose the spot.** Flat, a little higher than the ground around it so rain runs away, and clear of stones and sticks. Never in a dry river bed.
2. **Lay the footprint** (sold separately as ACC-TENT-FOOTPRINT-2 for the 2-person tent) and the inner tent on it, door facing away from the wind.
3. **Assemble the poles** and match each pole's colour to the clips of the same colour on the inner tent. Push the pole ends into the corner eyelets.
4. **Clip the inner tent** onto the poles, from the bottom up.
5. **Throw the flysheet over**, match its colours to the poles, and buckle it at each corner.
6. **Peg it out.** Push pegs in at 45 degrees, leaning away from the tent. Tension the guy lines until the flysheet is tight and doesn't touch the inner tent anywhere; where they touch, water seeps through.

## In heavy rain

- Keep the flysheet tight; re-tension it after an hour, because wet fabric stretches.
- Open the vents at the top a little to cut condensation. Drops on the inside on a cold night are usually condensation, not a leak.
- Before your first trip, run seam sealer (ACC-SEAM-SEALER) along the flysheet seams for extra protection.

## Taking it down and storing it

Shake off water, and if you pack it wet, dry it fully at home within 24 hours. A tent stored damp grows mould, and mould is not covered by the warranty. Fold the poles from the middle outwards so the cord inside isn't strained.

## Something broken?

Poles, seams, zips or buckles that fail during normal setup in the first year are covered by our [warranty](warranty-claims).
