This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you, why, and for how long.

## What we keep

- **Who you are**: your name, email address and phone number.
- **Where to deliver**: the addresses you give us.
- **What you bought**: your orders, invoices, returns and refunds.
- **What you told us**: emails and WhatsApp messages to our support team.
- **Bank details**, only if you gave them to us for a cash-on-delivery refund.

We don't keep your card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or UPI PIN: payments go through our payment provider and never pass through our systems.

## Why

To deliver your orders, handle returns, refunds and warranty claims, answer your questions, and keep the records the law requires. If you agree to it at checkout, we also send you news about sales; every such email has an unsubscribe link.

## How long

| Data | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Order and invoice records | 8 years, because tax rules require it |
| Support emails and messages | 2 years after your last message |
| Bank details for a cash-on-delivery refund | 90 days after the refund is paid |
| Your account (name, saved addresses) | Until you ask us to delete it |

## Who sees it

Our support team, our warehouse team, the courier (your name, address and phone number, to deliver), and our payment provider. We never sell your data, and we never share one customer's details with another. Our support team will not tell anyone else about your order, even someone who knows your order ID, unless they write from the email address on the order.

## Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Email support@tidewater.example from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days. See [Your account and password](account-and-password).
