A Tidewater e-gift card is the easy present for someone who loves the outdoors.

## Buying a gift card

- E-gift cards come in three values: **₹1,000, ₹2,500 and ₹5,000**.
- We email the card to the person you choose, with your message, within an hour of payment. You can also pick a date for it to arrive, up to 3 months ahead.
- There is no shipping charge, because nothing is posted.
- Gift cards are never discounted, including during festival sales.

## Using a gift card

- Enter the 16-character code at checkout. You can use it on as many orders as you like until the balance runs out.
- If your order costs more than the card's balance, pay the rest with UPI, a card or net banking.
- A gift card can't be used to buy another gift card.
- Gift cards work for orders in India and abroad.

## How long it lasts

A gift card is valid for **12 months from the day it is sent**. The expiry date is in the gift card email. We can't extend it.

## Returns and refunds

Gift cards can't be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash. If you return something you paid for with a gift card, the refund goes back to the card's balance.

## Lost the email?

We can resend a gift card to the email address it was originally sent to. Email support@tidewater.example with the buyer's name and order ID. For your safety we can't send the code to a different address.
