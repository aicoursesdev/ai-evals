All our jackets use the same body measurements. Measure your chest at its fullest point, under your arms, with a soft tape, then find your size below.

| Size | Chest (cm) | Back length (cm) | Sleeve (cm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 88–96 | 70 | 61 |
| M | 96–104 | 72 | 63 |
| L | 104–112 | 74 | 65 |
| XL | 112–120 | 76 | 67 |

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one if you will wear a fleece or thick layer underneath.

## How each jacket fits

| Jacket | SKU | Fit | Sizes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coastal Light Shell | JKT-COASTAL | Slim: close to the body for walking and cycling | S, M, L, XL |
| Monsoon Rain Jacket | JKT-MONSOON | Regular: room for a T-shirt and a light fleece | S, M, L, XL |
| Storm Pro Jacket | JKT-STORM | Relaxed: room for a warm layer, longer at the back | M, L, XL |

The size goes at the end of the SKU: a medium Monsoon Rain Jacket is JKT-MONSOON-M.

## Hoods and sleeves

Every hood adjusts at the back and fits over a cap. Sleeves end at the wrist with your arms by your side, and have cuffs you can tighten so rain doesn't run in when you lift your arms.

## Still not sure?

- If you are usually an M in T-shirts, you are usually an M in our jackets too.
- The Coastal Light Shell is the snuggest; many people size up in it.
- Email support@tidewater.example with your height and chest measurement and we will suggest a size.

If it doesn't fit, you can return it unused, with its tags, within 30 days of delivery, and order the right size. See [Returns](returns-policy).
