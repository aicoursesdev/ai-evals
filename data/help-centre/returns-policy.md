Changed your mind, or it doesn't fit? You can return most things within 30 days of delivery.

## What you can return

An item can come back if:

- it reaches us within 30 days of the day it was delivered (the delivery date on your order page counts as day 0),
- it is unused, with its tags still on, and
- it is not on the list of things we can't take back.

We can't take back gift cards, personalised items (anything with a name patch or embroidery), or bottles and pouches once they have been used. An unused bottle or pouch, still in its packaging, is fine to return.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in, open **Orders**, choose the order and press **Return an item**. If you checked out as a guest, email support@tidewater.example with your order ID (it looks like TW-10423) and the item you are sending back.
2. Pack the item with its tags, in its original packaging if you still have it.
3. **In India**, pickup is free: a courier collects the parcel from your address within 3 business days. You don't need to print anything.
4. **Outside India**, please send the parcel yourself to the address below with a tracked service. International customers pay the return shipping.

Tidewater Goods Returns
Unit 4, Harbour Road Industrial Estate
Kochi, Kerala 682003, India

## What happens next

When the parcel reaches our warehouse in Kochi, we check that the item is unused and has its tags. If it passes, we refund you; see [Refunds: when and where your money goes](refunds). If it doesn't pass, we send it back to you and explain why.

## Exchanges

We don't swap items directly. To change a size or colour, return the item for a refund and place a new order; that way the new one ships straight away instead of waiting for the return. The [jacket size guide](jacket-size-guide) may save you a return.

## Damaged or wrong item?

That isn't a normal return: please tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. See [Damaged or wrong item](damaged-or-wrong-item).
