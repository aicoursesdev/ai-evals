Opened your parcel and something isn't there? Before you write, two quick checks.

## First, check

1. **Is there a second parcel?** Large orders, such as a tent with other items, sometimes ship in two parcels. Your shipping email lists a tracking link for each one.
2. **Look inside everything.** Small things like seam sealer or a document pouch are sometimes tucked inside a backpack or a dry bag to protect them.

## Then, tell us within 48 hours

If it still isn't there, email support@tidewater.example **within 48 hours of delivery** with:

- your order ID (it looks like TW-10423),
- which item is missing, and
- a photo of the parcel and its shipping label.

We check the weight our warehouse recorded when your parcel was packed and what the courier recorded, and reply within one business day.

## What we do

Once we have checked, you choose:

- we send the missing item again at no cost, or
- we refund it in full to the way you paid, including its share of shipping.

## The whole parcel is missing

If tracking says **Delivered** but no parcel came, see [Tracking your order](track-order). We open a trace with the courier, and if the parcel is lost we send everything again or refund you in full.
