We ship from our warehouse in Kochi to every PIN code our courier partners serve.

## Prices and times

| Service | Price | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ₹79, free on orders over ₹1,500 | 4–7 business days |
| Express | ₹199 | 2–3 business days, Kochi, Bengaluru and Chennai only |

Free shipping is worked out on the order total after any discount code. Express orders must be placed before 11:00 to leave the same day.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹5,000, with a ₹40 handling fee.

## Tracking

We send a tracking number by SMS when your parcel ships. Enter it on the courier's website to see where it is.

## Delays

During the monsoon some routes in Kerala and the Western Ghats can take a few days longer. If your parcel is more than 3 days late, write to us and we will look into it.
