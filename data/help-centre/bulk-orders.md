Kitting out a trekking group, a school trip or your team's offsite? We are happy to help with larger orders.

## What counts as a bulk order

An order of **10 or more of the same product**, or anything over ₹50,000 in one order. Below that, simply order on the website.

## Discounts

| Quantity of one product | Discount |
|---|---|
| 10 to 24 | 5% off |
| 25 or more | 10% off |

Bulk discounts can't be combined with festival sale prices.

## Your logo

We can print your logo on dry bags and backpacks for orders of **25 or more of one product**. Printing adds about a week. Items with a logo are personalised, so they can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or wrong.

## How to ask for a quote

Email bulk@tidewater.example with:

- the products and quantities (sizes too, for jackets and sandals),
- the delivery address and the date you need them by, and
- your company's name and GSTIN, if you want a GST invoice for input credit.

We reply with a quote within 2 business days.

## Paying and delivery

Bulk orders are paid by bank transfer before we pack them. Allow 2 to 3 weeks for delivery, or about 3 to 4 weeks with logo printing. We ship bulk orders within India only.

## Returns

Unused items without a logo can be returned within 30 days of delivery, like any order. For returns of more than 10 items, please email us first so we can arrange a single pickup.
