Moved, or spotted a typo in your address? We can change it until your order is packed.

## Before your order is packed

While your order's status is **Placed**, email support@tidewater.example or message us on WhatsApp (+91 90000 01234) with:

- your order ID (it looks like TW-10423),
- the full new address, including the PIN code, and
- a phone number the courier can call.

We confirm by email once it is changed. If the new address is in a different city, the shipping charge may change; express delivery is only available to some cities (see [Express delivery](express-delivery)).

## After your order is packed

Once the order is **Packed**, the label is printed and the parcel is handed to the courier, so we can't change the address any more. You can:

- use the courier's tracking page to ask them to hold the parcel or try again another day, or
- if it can't be delivered, the courier brings it back to us and we refund you once it arrives.

## Changing your phone number

You can change the phone number on an order at any time; just email us. The courier uses it to call you on the day.

## Your saved addresses

To change the addresses saved in your account for next time, go to **Account** and then **Addresses**. Changing a saved address doesn't change orders you have already placed.
