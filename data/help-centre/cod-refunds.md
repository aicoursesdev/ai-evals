If you paid cash on delivery, there is no card or UPI account for us to send your refund back to, so we pay it into a bank account by bank transfer (NEFT).

## What we need from you

Reply to the email from our support team with:

- the account holder's name, as the bank has it,
- the account number, and
- the bank's IFSC (the 11-character branch code on your cheque book or banking app, four letters, then a zero, then six letters or digits).

The account should be in the name of the person who placed the order. Please type the details rather than sending a photo of a cheque or passbook.

We will never ask for your card number, PIN, OTP, UPI PIN or net banking password. If someone claiming to be Tidewater asks for any of these, don't reply, and tell us at support@tidewater.example.

## When the money arrives

The transfer is made **5–7 business days after your return reaches our warehouse and passes inspection**, the same as every other refund. If your details are incomplete or the bank rejects the transfer, we email you, and the 5–7 days start again once we have the right details.

## Keeping your details safe

Send bank details only by replying to our email, never on social media or in a review. We delete the bank details 90 days after the refund is paid. See [Your data and how long we keep it](privacy).
