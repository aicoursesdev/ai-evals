Outside India, we ship to the United Arab Emirates, Singapore and the United Kingdom. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Prices and times

International shipping is a flat price per order, set by the country:

| Country | Shipping per order | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| United Arab Emirates | ₹1,499 | 7–14 business days |
| Singapore | ₹1,799 | 7–14 business days |
| United Kingdom | ₹2,299 | 7–14 business days |

There is no free shipping on international orders, and express delivery is not available abroad.

## Customs duties and taxes

Your country may charge import duties and taxes on your parcel. **You pay these to the courier on delivery**; they are not included in our prices or shipping charge. We can't tell you the exact amount in advance, because your country's customs office sets it. If you refuse to pay them, the parcel comes back to us and we refund the items but not the shipping.

## Paying

International orders are paid by credit or debit card, or with a [gift card](gift-cards). Cash on delivery, UPI, net banking and EMI are only available in India.

## Returns from abroad

You can return items within 30 days of delivery, like everyone else, but international customers pay the return shipping. Send the parcel to our Kochi warehouse with a tracked service; the address is in [Returns](returns-policy). We can't refund duties or taxes you paid on import; your country's customs office may be able to.

## Damaged or wrong items abroad

If an item arrives damaged or is the wrong one, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, just as in India. We pay the return shipping in that case. See [Damaged or wrong item](damaged-or-wrong-item).
