Every order goes through four steps, and you can see which one yours is at on your **Orders** page.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Placed | We have your order and payment. You can still cancel or change the address. |
| Packed | Packed and waiting for the courier, usually within 12 hours of ordering. It can't be cancelled any more. |
| Shipped | With the courier. You have a tracking link by email and SMS. |
| Delivered | The courier has marked it delivered. Return windows count from this date. |

## Your tracking link

When your order ships, we email and text you a tracking link. Guests can also track an order from the **Track an order** page with the order ID (it looks like TW-10423) and the email address used at checkout.

The courier's tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after we hand the parcel over, so a link that says "no information yet" on the first day is normal.

## When to contact us

Please email support@tidewater.example with your order ID if:

- the tracking hasn't changed for 3 business days,
- your order is past its delivery time (3–6 business days for standard, 1–2 for express, 7–14 for international), or
- the tracking says **Delivered** but you don't have the parcel. Check with neighbours and your building's security first; then tell us within 48 hours.

We open a trace with the courier, which usually takes up to 3 business days. If the parcel is lost, we send the items again or refund you in full, including shipping.

## Orders in more than one parcel

Large orders, such as a tent with other items, sometimes ship in two parcels. The shipping email says so and gives a tracking link for each.
