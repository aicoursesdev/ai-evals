We ship from our warehouse in Kochi to addresses across India.

## Prices and times

| Service | Price | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ₹99, free on orders over ₹2,000 | 3–6 business days |
| Express | ₹249 | 1–2 business days, metro cities only |

Business days are Monday to Saturday, except public holidays. The time is counted from when your order leaves our warehouse, and we pack most orders within 12 hours of you placing them.

## Free standard shipping

Standard shipping is free when the items in your order add up to more than ₹2,000, after any sale discount. An order of exactly ₹2,000 pays ₹99. Free shipping applies to standard delivery only; express always costs ₹249. E-gift cards are sent by email, so they never have a shipping charge.

## Express delivery

Express reaches Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad and Kochi in 1–2 business days. Checkout only offers it when your PIN code is in one of these cities. See [Express delivery](express-delivery).

## Cash on delivery

You can pay cash on delivery anywhere we ship in India, for orders up to ₹10,000. See [Ways to pay](payment-methods).

## Where is my parcel?

When your order ships we email and text you a tracking link. See [Tracking your order](track-order). If it is later than the delivery time above, tell us and we will chase the courier.

## Shipping abroad

We also ship to the UAE, Singapore and the UK. See [International shipping and customs duties](international-shipping).
