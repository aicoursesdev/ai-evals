Sorry about that. If something arrives damaged, faulty out of the box, or isn't what you ordered, we will put it right.

## Tell us within 48 hours

Email support@tidewater.example **within 48 hours of delivery** with:

- your order ID (it looks like TW-10423),
- a photo of the item showing the damage, or of the wrong item with its label, and
- a photo of the outer box if it was damaged too.

Reports after 48 hours are handled as normal returns, so the item then has to be unused, with tags, and within 30 days of delivery (see [Returns](returns-policy)). Faults that appear later on a jacket, backpack or tent may be covered by the [warranty](warranty-claims).

## What we do

You choose:

- **a replacement**, sent as soon as one is in stock, or
- **a full refund**, including the shipping you paid, back to the way you paid.

## Do I need to send it back?

- **Items under ₹500** (for example a phone pouch, a document pouch or seam sealer): no. Keep it or recycle it; we don't need it back.
- **Items of ₹500 or more**: yes, but you don't pay. In India a courier collects it for free; abroad we pay the return shipping.

For a replacement, we send the new item when the courier has collected the old one. For a refund, the money arrives 5–7 business days after the item reaches our warehouse, or after we approve your photo for items under ₹500.

## Wrong size?

If you ordered the wrong size, that's a normal return, not a wrong item. If we sent a different size from the one on your order, it's a wrong item and this page applies.
