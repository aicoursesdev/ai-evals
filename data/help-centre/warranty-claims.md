Our tents, backpacks and jackets carry a **1-year warranty** against manufacturing defects, counted from the day your order was delivered.

## What the warranty covers

Faults in how the item was made, such as:

- **seams** that come apart or leak along the stitching,
- **zips** that split, lose teeth or stop closing,
- **buckles** and clips that crack or won't lock,
- tent poles or pole sleeves that fail during normal setup.

## What it doesn't cover

- Normal wear and tear: fading, worn fabric, a waterproof coating that has thinned after years of use.
- Misuse or accidents: burns from a stove or campfire, tears from snagging on rocks or barbed wire, damage from a washing machine or tumble dryer, bleach.
- Items that have been altered or repaired by someone else.
- Products other than tents, backpacks and jackets. Dry bags, sandals, bottles, pouches and accessories have no warranty beyond our 30-day returns and our help with [damaged or wrong items](damaged-or-wrong-item).

## How to claim

Email support@tidewater.example with:

1. your **order ID** (it looks like TW-10423; it is in your order confirmation email),
2. **photos** of the fault, close up and of the whole item, and
3. a photo of the product label inside the item.

A claim needs the order ID and photos; without them we can't start it. If you have lost the order ID, tell us the email address you ordered with and roughly when.

## What happens next

We reply within 2 business days. If the fault is covered, we repair the item or send you a replacement. In India a courier collects the item for free; from abroad, please ask us before sending anything. If we can't repair or replace it, we offer a refund, which a member of our team approves.

Taking care of your gear helps it last: see [Caring for your waterproof backpack](backpack-care) and [Setting up your tent](tent-setup).
