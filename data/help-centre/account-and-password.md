You don't need an account to shop with us; you can check out as a guest. An account lets you see all your orders, start returns in a click and save addresses.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address you signed up with.
3. We email you a link to set a new password. It works for 30 minutes, and only once.

No email? Check your spam folder, and make sure it is the address you signed up with. If you checked out as a guest, you don't have a password: create an account with the same email and your past orders appear in it.

## Changing your email address

Go to **Account**, then **Profile**, and enter the new address. We send a link to the new address to confirm it. If you can't sign in to the old address any more, email support@tidewater.example from the new address with your name, phone number and a recent order ID, and we will help.

## Locked out?

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, then use **Forgot password**.

## Deleting your account

Email support@tidewater.example from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. We have to keep order and invoice records for tax reasons even after the account is gone; see [Your data and how long we keep it](privacy).

## Keeping your account safe

We will never ask for your password, by email, phone or WhatsApp. If you get a message asking for it, it isn't from us.
