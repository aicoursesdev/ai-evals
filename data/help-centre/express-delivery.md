Need your gear before the weekend trek? Express delivery gets it to you in 1–2 business days.

## Where express goes

Express is available in these metro cities only:

- Mumbai
- Delhi NCR
- Bengaluru
- Chennai
- Hyderabad
- Kolkata
- Pune
- Ahmedabad
- Kochi

Checkout offers express only when your PIN code is in one of them. Everywhere else in India gets standard delivery (3–6 business days).

## Price

Express costs **₹249** per order, whatever the order is worth. Free shipping on orders over ₹2,000 applies to standard delivery only.

## Timing

- Express orders placed before 12:00 IST, Monday to Saturday, are packed and leave our warehouse the same day.
- Orders placed after 12:00, or on a Sunday or public holiday, leave the next business day.
- Delivery takes 1–2 business days after that. Couriers don't deliver express parcels on Sundays.

So an express order placed at 10:00 on a Friday usually arrives on Saturday or Monday.

## If express is late

If your express parcel hasn't arrived within 2 business days of leaving our warehouse, email us with your order ID. We will chase the courier and tell you what happened.

## Changing to express

You can't switch an order to express after you have placed it. If it is still **Placed** (not yet packed), you can cancel it and order again with express; see [Cancelling an order](cancel-order).
