Twice a year we hold a festival sale: the **Onam Sale** in August and the **Diwali Sale** in October and November.

| Sale | Dates (2026) |
|---|---|
| Onam Sale | 18 to 27 August |
| Diwali Sale | 30 October to 8 November |

## The discount

During a sale, everything is **20% off** except gift cards. The sale price is shown on each product page and in your basket, rounded down to the rupee. You don't need a code.

## Sale prices apply only during the sale

- An order gets the sale price only if it is placed between 00:00 IST on the first day and 23:59 IST on the last day of the sale.
- We don't match prices, ours or anyone else's.
- If you bought something before a sale starts, or after it ends, we can't refund the difference. If you bought it recently and it is unused, you can return it within 30 days and buy it again in the sale, as long as it's still in stock.
- Sale discounts can't be combined with other discount codes.

## Returns and warranty

Sale items follow our normal rules: returns within 30 days of delivery, unused and with tags, and the usual warranty on tents, backpacks and jackets. Refunds are for the sale price you paid.

## Delivery during a sale

We get many more orders during a sale, so packing can take up to a day longer than our usual 12 hours. Delivery times start when the order leaves our warehouse.

## Stock

Sale stock is limited. An item in your basket isn't reserved until you pay.
