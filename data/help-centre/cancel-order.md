You can cancel an order for free until we pack it. We usually pack orders within 12 hours of you placing them, so if you want to cancel, do it quickly.

## How to cancel

- Sign in, open **Orders**, choose the order and press **Cancel order**. The button shows only while the order's status is **Placed**.
- Checked out as a guest? Email support@tidewater.example or message us on WhatsApp at +91 90000 01234 with your order ID. We act on cancellations in the order they arrive during support hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST).

## Your money

- **Prepaid orders** (UPI, card, net banking, EMI or gift card): the refund starts the day you cancel and reaches you within 5–7 business days, back to the way you paid.
- **Cash on delivery**: nothing was paid, so there is nothing to refund.

## Already packed?

Once an order's status is **Packed** or **Shipped**, we can't stop it. Let it arrive, then return it within 30 days of delivery. In India the return pickup is free. See [Returns](returns-policy).

## Changing instead of cancelling

If you only want a different size or address, you don't always need to cancel. Before packing we can change the delivery address (see [Changing your delivery address](change-address)). To change a size, cancel and place a new order while the order is still Placed.
