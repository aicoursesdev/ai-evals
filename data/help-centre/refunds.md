Here is how refunds work once you have sent something back or cancelled an order.

## When you get your money

We refund within **5–7 business days after your return reaches our warehouse and passes inspection**. Business days are Monday to Saturday, except public holidays. Inspection means we check that the item is unused and has its tags (see [Returns](returns-policy)).

If you cancelled an order before it was packed, there is nothing to inspect: a prepaid order's refund starts the day you cancel and arrives within 5–7 business days.

Every refund is checked and approved by a member of our support team before it is paid. We never pay refunds automatically.

## Where the money goes

Refunds always go back to the way you paid. We can't send them anywhere else, such as a different card, a friend's account or a different UPI ID.

| You paid with | Your refund goes to |
|---|---|
| UPI | The same UPI account |
| Credit or debit card | The same card |
| Net banking | The same bank account |
| EMI | Your credit card; your bank closes the EMI plan |
| Gift card | The gift card's balance |
| Cash on delivery | A bank account you give us; see [Refunds for cash-on-delivery orders](cod-refunds) |

Your bank may take a few extra days to show a card refund on your statement. If 7 business days have passed since we told you the refund was sent, email us with your order ID and we will send you the bank's reference number.

## How much you get back

We refund the price you paid for the item, including any sale discount you had. The original shipping charge is refunded only if the item arrived damaged, was the wrong item, or was missing. If you return part of an order, you get back the price of the items you returned.

## Price drops

We don't refund the difference if a price drops after you buy, including during a festival sale. See [Festival sale terms](festival-sale-terms).
