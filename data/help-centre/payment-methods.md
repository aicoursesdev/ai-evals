You can pay for orders in India in five ways. International orders are paid by credit or debit card.

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| UPI | Any UPI app. Approve the request in your app within 10 minutes. |
| Credit or debit card | Visa, Mastercard and RuPay, issued in India or abroad. |
| Net banking | Most Indian banks. |
| Cash on delivery (COD) | India only, orders up to ₹10,000. Pay the courier in cash or by UPI at the door. |
| EMI | On credit cards, for orders of ₹3,000 or more, over 3, 6 or 9 months. Your bank sets the interest. |

You can also use a [gift card](gift-cards), on its own or together with another method.

## Payment failed but money was taken?

Sometimes a UPI or card payment fails at our end after your bank has taken the money. The bank reverses it automatically, usually within 5–7 business days. If it hasn't come back after 7 business days, email us with the date, the amount and the UPI reference number (UTR) or the last four digits of the card, and we will trace it.

## Charged twice?

If you see two charges for one order, one of them is usually a payment that failed and will be reversed as above. If both stay for more than 7 business days, email us with both references and we will refund the extra one.

## Invoices

Every order's GST invoice is attached to the shipping email and can be downloaded from **Orders**. To claim GST input credit for a business, enter your GSTIN at checkout; we can't add it to an invoice afterwards.

## Keeping your money safe

We never ask for your card number, PIN, OTP or UPI PIN by email, phone or WhatsApp. Payments go through our payment provider, and we never see or store your full card number. See [Your data and how long we keep it](privacy).
