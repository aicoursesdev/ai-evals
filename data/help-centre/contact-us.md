We are a small team in Kochi, and real people read every message.

## How to reach us

| Way | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Email | support@tidewater.example | Anything, especially returns, refunds and warranty claims with photos |
| WhatsApp | +91 90000 01234 | Quick questions, cancellations and address changes |
| Bulk orders | bulk@tidewater.example | Quotes for 10 or more of one product |

We don't take phone calls yet. Please don't send order details or bank details through social media messages.

## When we answer

Support hours are **Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST**. We aim to reply within one business day. Messages sent on Sunday are answered on Monday, so Monday replies can take a little longer.

## Help us help you faster

- Put your **order ID** in the subject (it looks like TW-10423).
- Write from the email address you ordered with. For your privacy, we can only discuss an order with the email address on it.
- For damage, wrong items and warranty claims, attach photos.
- One email per issue is fine; we will reply to all of them.

## Visiting

Our warehouse is at Unit 4, Harbour Road Industrial Estate, Kochi, Kerala 682003. It isn't a shop, so we can't take walk-in visits, collections or returns over the counter.

## Complaints

If we have got something wrong, tell us. Email support@tidewater.example with "Complaint" in the subject and our support lead will reply personally within 2 business days.
